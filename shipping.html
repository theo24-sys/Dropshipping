Shipping policy

Last updated 20 November 2026. This policy explains how MORGGY orders are shipped, what delivery estimates mean and who pays import charges.

1. Where we ship

MORGGY currently accepts orders for delivery to Singapore and Malaysia. Other destinations, including Vietnam, are listed as coming soon and cannot be ordered. Checkout blocks unsupported destinations; we do not accept an order we cannot fulfil.

2. Fulfilment model

MORGGY does not hold inventory. Products are supplied by our supplier platform, CJdropshipping, and shipped directly from the supplier's facility to your address. A single order may be split into more than one parcel if the items are held at different supplier locations; each parcel gets its own tracking number at no extra shipping cost to you.

4. Shipping charges

Shipping is calculated from the destination and the number of items in the order, and is shown in the cart and at checkout before payment. We do not offer free shipping, because the cost of international postage is real and would otherwise be built into product prices.

5. Tracking

Every parcel ships with a tracking number, emailed to the address given at checkout when the supplier dispatches. Tracking can take up to 48 hours to appear in the carrier's system. You can also check status on the tracking page.

6. Import taxes, duties and customs

Prices on this site exclude import taxes, duties and customs handling fees. These are set by the destination authority and are the responsibility of the recipient:

  • Singapore: Singapore Customs may apply GST and duties depending on the goods and their value. Where a parcel is assessed, the courier collects the amount before delivery.
  • Malaysia: The Royal Malaysian Customs Department may apply sales tax and duties, including on low-value imported goods. Any charge is collected by the courier.

Refusing a parcel to avoid customs charges means the parcel is returned or destroyed by the carrier. In that case we can refund the product value only after the supplier confirms the return, and shipping is not refundable.

7. Addresses and delivery attempts

Please give a complete local address and a reachable mobile number. Singapore orders need a six-digit postcode and unit number; Malaysian orders need the postcode, city and state. If a parcel is returned because the address was incomplete or nobody could receive it, re-shipping is charged at the standard rate.

8. Delays and lost parcels

If a parcel is more than five working days past its estimate, contact us and we will open a case with the carrier. If a parcel is confirmed lost, we replace the item or refund it in full. Customs holds and severe weather are outside our control, but we still chase them for you.

9. Restricted items

We do not list products that cannot legally be imported into Singapore or Malaysia. Lithium batteries in electronics are shipped by the supplier under carrier rules for battery-containing goods; if a carrier refuses a specific item, you are offered a substitute or a full refund.
