Frequently asked questions

If your question is not answered here, contact us and we will write back within one working day.

Orders and payment

Which countries do you deliver to?

Singapore and Malaysia are open now. Other destinations, including Vietnam, are not yet supported and cannot be ordered — checkout blocks them rather than taking an order we cannot fulfil. Join the newsletter to hear when a market opens.

What currency am I charged in?

All orders are settled in US dollars. Prices are converted for display into Singapore dollars or Malaysian ringgit using the rate published in our store settings, which is shown on product pages and at checkout. The converted figure is an estimate; the USD total is the amount charged.

Which payment methods can I use?

Payments will run through Paystack and Flutterwave, which accept local cards and bank transfer methods in both markets. Card details are entered on the provider's secure page, never on this website. Payments are being finalised at launch; until they are live, checkout states that no order can be completed.

Do you store my card details?

No. Card data is handled entirely by the payment provider. Our servers only store the gateway reference, the amount and the payment status.

When is my order sent to the supplier?

Only after the payment provider confirms the payment on our server. An order without cleared payment is never submitted to a supplier.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Estimates by destination are 5–9 working days for Singapore and 6–10 working days for Malaysia, counted from cleared payment. They include supplier preparation and international transit. These are estimates, not guarantees — customs checks can add time.

Is shipping free?

No, and we do not advertise it as free. Shipping is priced per destination and by item count, and it is shown in the cart and at checkout before you pay.

Will I pay customs or taxes?

Import charges are set by your customs authority and are not included in our prices. Singapore GST and Malaysian sales tax may apply to imported goods depending on value and category. If a parcel is assessed, the courier collects the amount before delivery. We cannot estimate or prepay these charges.

How do I track my parcel?

A tracking number is emailed when the supplier dispatches. Tracking can take up to 48 hours to appear with the carrier. Use the tracking page or email us if you do not receive one within three working days of dispatch.

Products and suppliers

Where do the products come from?

From CJdropshipping, a supplier platform based in China. We map each MORGGY product to a specific supplier item, keep the supplier reference on file, and confirm stock before an order is accepted.

Do you hold stock?

No. MORGGY owns no inventory and operates no warehouse. The supplier packs and ships each order directly to the customer.

Are the product details verified?

Not yet, and the site says so. Every product is still marked as unmapped to a supplier item, with stock shown as unconfirmed, because the CJdropshipping mapping is not finished. When a product is mapped, its supplier reference and a confirmed stock level replace those notices. We do not publish specifications we have not checked with the supplier.

Are the reviews real?

There are no reviews shown, because no customer reviews have been collected yet. We will only display reviews written by verified buyers.

Returns

What if my item arrives damaged or wrong?

Email us within 7 days of delivery with photographs. We arrange a replacement or a full refund, including shipping. You normally do not need to return low-value items to China.

Can I return something I changed my mind about?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. Return postage to the supplier's facility is paid by you. The full policy is on the returns page.
