Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026. This policy explains what personal data MORGGY collects, why, and who it is shared with.

1. Who we are

MORGGY is an online store operated by Morggy Technologies, Nairobi, Kenya. For the personal data described here, Morggy Technologies is the data controller. Contact: hello@morggy.com.

2. What we collect

We do not collect or store card numbers, CVV codes or bank credentials. Those are entered on the payment provider's page and never reach our servers.

3. Cart and preferences stored in your browser

Your cart and selected country are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them when you return. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us by the storefront, and can be cleared by emptying your cart or clearing site data in your browser.

We do not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If analytics are added later, this page will list them before they are enabled.

4. Who we share data with

  • Suppliers and fulfilment. Our supplier platform, CJdropshipping, receives the recipient name, delivery address, phone number and order contents so the parcel can be shipped. Suppliers receive only what is needed to fulfil the order.
  • Payment providers. Paystack and Flutterwave process payments and receive the amount, currency, your email and a payment reference. Their own privacy policies apply to card data.
  • Carriers. The courier receives the delivery details needed to deliver and to contact you about the parcel.
  • Service providers. Hosting, email and error-monitoring providers process data on our instructions.
  • Authorities. Where required by law, customs or a valid legal request.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

5. International transfers

Orders are fulfilled from China and our systems may be hosted outside Singapore and Malaysia. Where data leaves your country, we rely on the transfer mechanisms permitted by applicable law and keep transfers limited to what fulfilment requires.

6. How long we keep data

  • Order records: up to 7 years, for tax, accounting and warranty purposes.
  • Support messages: up to 24 months after the issue is closed.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.
  • Technical logs: up to 12 months.

7. Your rights

Subject to local law — including Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act and Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act — you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent for marketing. Write to hello@morggy.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in the email.

8. Security

The store is served over HTTPS. Access to order data is limited to the people who need it to fulfil orders and handle support. Payment confirmation is verified on our server before an order is sent to a supplier. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to the minimum needed to run the store.

9. Children

MORGGY is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it and material changes are announced on the site. Questions or complaints: hello@morggy.com or the contact page.
