The store

Fewer things, chosen properly.

MORGGY is a small international retailer run by Morggy Technologies in Nairobi, Kenya. We sell a short list of everyday products, sourced from overseas suppliers, and delivered to customers in Singapore and Malaysia.

Why we started

Shopping across borders is usually a choice between two bad options: a marketplace where the same product appears a hundred times with no accountability, or a big retailer whose range is built for someone else's climate and habits.

We wanted a third option — a store that stocks nothing it cannot stand behind, shows what a product actually costs, and tells you honestly how long delivery will take. A considered selection for customers in Singapore and Malaysia.

How an order works

  1. You order. Choose your country, product and variant, and proceed to payment.
  2. Payment verification. Payment is verified through our payment provider. Orders are only processed after payment clears.
  3. Supplier availability. The item is confirmed with the supplier. If unavailable, the order is cancelled and promptly refunded in full.
  4. Supplier dispatch. The supplier packs and dispatches the parcel directly to your address.
  5. Tracking update. Tracking details are emailed to you once assigned by the carrier.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish reviews or ratings that customers have not written. None are shown until real reviews exist.
  • We do not label products best sellers without order data to support it.
  • Delivery details for Singapore and Malaysia are to be confirmed. [Owner to confirm]
  • We do not store card details. Payments are handled entirely by the payment provider.
