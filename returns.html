Returns and refunds

Last updated 20 November 2026. If something arrives wrong, we fix it. This page sets out exactly how.

1. The short version

  • Damaged, faulty or incorrect items: report within 7 days of delivery. We replace or refund in full, shipping included.
  • Changed your mind: return within 14 days of delivery, unused and in original packaging. Return postage is yours.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 working days of approval.
  • We never ask you to send card details, and we never refund to a different account than the one that paid.

2. Items that arrive damaged or faulty

Email orders@morggy.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photographs of the packaging and the item. We will confirm within one working day and offer a replacement or a full refund, including the shipping you paid. For low-value items we usually issue the refund without asking you to post anything back; where a return is needed, we cover the postage.

3. Wrong item received

If the parcel contains a different product, colour or size from what you ordered, treat it as a damaged item: report within 7 days with photographs and we will correct it at our cost.

4. Change of mind

You may return an unused item within 14 days of delivery, in its original packaging and with all parts. Email us first so we can give you the supplier's return address. Return postage to China is paid by you and is not refunded; original outbound shipping is also not refunded for change-of-mind returns. Once the item reaches the supplier and is checked, we refund the product value.

Because returns cross an international border, we recommend a tracked service. We cannot refund items that are lost on the way back without proof of postage.

5. What cannot be returned

  • Items marked final sale or clearance at the time of purchase.
  • Products used for personal hygiene where the seal is broken, such as pet grooming tools once used.
  • Items returned after the 14-day window, or without the original packaging.
  • Items damaged by misuse, modification or normal wear.

6. Cancellations

An order can be cancelled for a full refund at any point before the supplier dispatches it. Once dispatched, the return process above applies. If the supplier cannot fulfil an item after payment — for example because stock ran out — we cancel that line and refund it in full, and will offer a substitute if you want one.

Refunds are issued in US dollars for the amount originally charged. Any difference caused by exchange rates or your bank's foreign transaction fee is outside our control and is not reimbursed.

8. Faulty electricals

Electronics carry a 30-day functional guarantee from delivery: if a device does not power on or fails in normal use, we replace it or refund it. Beyond 30 days, please contact us and we will approach the supplier for a remedy where one is available.

9. Customs charges on returns

Import charges already paid to a customs authority are not refundable by MORGGY. If an item is refused at customs and returned to the supplier, we refund the product value once the supplier confirms receipt, excluding shipping and any duties.

10. How to start a return

Email orders@morggy.com with: order number, item and variant, the reason, and photographs where relevant. We reply with next steps within one working day. You can also use the contact form.
