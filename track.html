What each status means

  • Awaiting payment — the order exists but no cleared payment has been received.
  • Payment verified — the gateway has confirmed the money. Nothing is sent to the supplier before this step.
  • Sent to supplier — the supplier has accepted the order and is preparing it.
  • Dispatched — the parcel has left the supplier with a tracking number.
  • In transit — the parcel is with the international carrier.
  • Delivered — the courier has recorded delivery at your address.

Tracking numbers can take up to 48 hours to appear in the carrier's system after dispatch. If a parcel is more than five working days past its estimate, contact us and we will open a case with the carrier.

Lookups read the live order record. If no parcel has been handed to a carrier yet, the page says so instead of showing a tracking number.
