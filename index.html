Shipping information

How delivery works

Your order is passed to the supplier within one working day of cleared payment. They dispatch from their facility, and the courier carries the parcel to your address. You receive a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves.

Import taxes and duties are collected by the destination customs authority and are not part of our prices. Any charge is shown by the courier before delivery.
